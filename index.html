All three panels above read from one shared Supabase cache, refreshed every 90 seconds by a background worker — not fetched directly by your browser. That means any number of people can have this open at once without multiplying API calls or hitting rate limits. This page re-reads that cache every 90s; toggle AUTO: OFF to stop and read manually instead. Solana rows may show a 🎯 KOL BUY badge — that means a tracked KOL bought it recently on MadeOnSol's free-tier feed, not that they're confirmed to still be holding it. Top signals may also show a 📡 badge (Galaxy Score + sentiment, hover for social dominance, 24h interactions, and top creators), a separate 📈/📉 AltRank badge (rank velocity, not a static score), and — only when genuinely spiking — an organic/mixed/manufactured hype verdict badge (✅/⚠️/🤖, spam-lift and creator-concentration math ported from a real open-source classifier by Nicki Sanders) once LunarCrush is upgraded to a paid plan — no free API tier exists for it. No badge at all means "not spiking," not "checked and clean." Solana rows also carry a RugCheck safety badge (🟢 safe / 🟡 caution / 🔴 danger, free, no key) — deliberately kept separate from the score above, since a token can score high on momentum and still be a rug; don't let a good score wave off a red badge.
